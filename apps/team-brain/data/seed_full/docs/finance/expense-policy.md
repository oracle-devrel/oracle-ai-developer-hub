# Expense policy

Owner: Bob (Finance).

## Travel

- Flights: economy under 6 hours, economy-plus/premium allowed above 6
  hours with manager approval.
- Hotels: $250/night cap in major metros, $180/night elsewhere. Book
  through the company travel portal when possible.
- Per diem: $75/day for meals when traveling, no receipts required under
  $75; itemize above that.

## Client entertainment

Sales and customer success can expense client meals/entertainment up to
$150/person without pre-approval; above that needs a manager sign-off
before booking, not after. This is a different approval path from
discounting a contract — see `sales/deal-desk-policy.md` for that, they're
unrelated even though both cross Brian's desk.

## Software and subscriptions

Any recurring subscription over $50/month needs finance approval before
purchase, logged against the requesting team's budget. One-off purchases
under $200 can go on a corporate card without pre-approval; expense within
30 days.

## Reimbursement timeline

Submitted expenses are reimbursed on the next bi-weekly payroll run, not
immediately — plan travel spend accordingly. Expenses submitted more than
60 days after the purchase date require a VP exception, per the same
approval chain as an out-of-policy vendor payment term
(`finance/vendor-payment-terms.md`).

## Questions

Route policy exceptions to Bob directly; day-to-day "is this covered"
questions can go in `#finance` first since someone's usually already asked
the same thing.
