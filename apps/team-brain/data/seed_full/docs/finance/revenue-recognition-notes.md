# Revenue recognition notes

Owner: Bob (Finance). Internal reference, not a substitute for talking to
our auditor on anything material.

## How we recognize

Annual contracts are recognized ratably over the contract term, not at
signing — a $60,000 annual contract signed in July recognizes $5,000/month
starting the month service begins, not $60,000 in July. This is why the
discount a rep offers (`sales/discount-matrix.md`) changes monthly
recognized revenue, not just the headline contract value, and why deal
desk logs the approved discount against the opportunity ID
(`sales/deal-desk-policy.md`) — finance ties that record to the invoice
schedule at close.

## Multi-year contracts

Recognized the same way — ratably over the full term, even though cash may
arrive annually or upfront depending on payment terms
(`finance/vendor-payment-terms.md` covers non-standard terms). A 3-year
deal doesn't front-load revenue just because a bigger cash payment landed
in year one.

## Usage-based add-ons

Metered usage (overage billing beyond a plan's included volume) is
recognized in the period it's incurred, not spread — this is the one place
our recognition isn't ratable, because the usage itself isn't ratable.

## Why this matters to non-finance teams

Sales sometimes assumes a signed deal immediately moves the quarter's
revenue number; it doesn't, it moves the contracted/booked number.
Marketing's webinar-sourced pipeline (`marketing/campaign-retro-q3-webinar.md`)
gets tracked as bookings influence, which is a different metric from
recognized revenue and shouldn't be conflated in board reporting.
