# Vendor payment terms

Owner: Bob (Finance). Covers both what we pay vendors on, and the process
for granting a customer non-standard payment terms.

## Our vendor terms

- Standard: Net-30 for any vendor under $5,000/month.
- Net-45 available for vendors over $5,000/month with a signed annual
  contract; requires Bob's sign-off.
- Net-60 only for strategic infrastructure vendors (cloud, database
  hosting) with multi-year commitments; requires Brian's sign-off, logged
  the same way a sales discount above 20% is (`sales/deal-desk-policy.md`)
  — same approval discipline, different direction of money.

## Customer-facing exceptions

Customers occasionally request quarterly billing or extended (Net-60)
payment terms instead of our standard annual-upfront. These route through
deal desk alongside the discount request, not separately — see
`sales/discount-matrix.md`, which already prices in a lower discount
ceiling for quarterly terms since the cash-flow timing has a real cost to
us.

## Why the same reviewer handles both

Bob reviews both incoming (vendor) and outgoing (customer) non-standard
terms because the underlying question is identical: does this terms
change create a cash-flow gap the business can't absorb this quarter. A
single reviewer catches the case where we're simultaneously extending
customer terms and tightening vendor terms in the same month, which
would otherwise go unnoticed by two different approvers.

## Escalation

Any request outside these bands (e.g. Net-90, or a customer wanting
milestone-based billing) goes to Brian directly, same escalation path as
a discount past the matrix ceiling.
