# Campaign retro: Q2 outbound sequence

Owner: Julia. Ran April-June 2026, targeted 400 mid-market accounts with a
5-touch email sequence plus one LinkedIn touch.

## Results

- Open rate: 31% (above the 24% company average)
- Reply rate: 3.1% (below the 5% target)
- Meetings booked: 9, of which 2 became opportunities in sales' pipeline
  (see `sales/deal-desk-policy.md` for how those got scoped)

## What worked

- The 2-hour post-touch follow-up (tested against next-day) converted
  2.3x better on reply rate. This is now the default across all campaigns,
  including the Q3 webinar follow-up (`marketing/launch-checklist-q3-webinar.md`).
- Subject lines referencing a specific pain point ("your close still takes
  9 days?") outperformed generic ones by roughly 2x open rate.

## What didn't work

- The LinkedIn touch added negligible lift (0.2 points of reply rate) for
  meaningful extra production time. Cut it from the Q3 sequence.
- Reply rate target of 5% was set from a benchmark deck, not our own prior
  data — recalibrating to 3.5% for Q3 based on this result.
