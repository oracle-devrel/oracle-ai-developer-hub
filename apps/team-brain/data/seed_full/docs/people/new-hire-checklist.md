# New hire checklist (HR side)

Owner: Henry (People). The engineering-specific technical ramp-up lives in
`engineering/onboarding-guide.md`; this is the HR/admin half that applies
to every new hire regardless of team.

## Before day 1

- [ ] Offer signed, start date confirmed, level/comp band matches the
      interview loop debrief (`people/interview-loop.md`)
- [ ] Laptop ordered (2-week lead time — order the moment the offer is
      signed, not the week before start)
- [ ] IT ticket filed for GitHub/Slack/email/HR system access, timed to
      be ready morning of day 1
- [ ] Manager assigned a 30/60/90 doc template

## Day 1

- [ ] HR paperwork (I-9 or equivalent, benefits enrollment window opens)
- [ ] Intro in `#general`, add to team Slack channels relevant to the role
- [ ] Manager 1:1, walk through the 30/60/90 doc
- [ ] Engineering hires: start `engineering/onboarding-guide.md`

## Week 1

- [ ] Benefits enrollment completed (30-day window from start date)
- [ ] PTO policy walkthrough (`people/pto-policy.md`), especially the
      on-call-week PTO norm if joining engineering
- [ ] First 1:1 with skip-level manager

## 90-day check-in

Henry runs a short survey at day 30, 60, and 90 covering onboarding
quality, manager support, and role clarity. Trends across new hires (not
individual responses) get reviewed quarterly with Brian — this is how the
onboarding guides above get revised when something's consistently
confusing.
