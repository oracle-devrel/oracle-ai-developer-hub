# PTO policy

Acme Ledger uses unlimited PTO with a 15-day recommended minimum per year,
tracked (not enforced) so managers can spot anyone under-using it.

## Requesting time off

- Under 3 days: Slack your manager, add it to the shared calendar. No
  formal approval needed, just visibility.
- 3 days or more: request at least 2 weeks ahead via the HR system so
  coverage can be arranged — for engineering, this means checking you're
  not on the on-call rotation that week (`ops/oncall-rotation.md`); swap
  with a teammate first if you are.
- Company holidays: 10 per year, listed in the HR system, plus the last
  week of December as a company-wide shutdown (skeleton on-call crew only,
  volunteers get 1.5x the usual on-call stipend).

## During critical periods

Engineering avoids scheduling PTO during a maintenance-window migration
week for ledger-core (see `engineering/adr-0007-event-sourced-ledger.md`)
if you're one of the people directly running it. Sales and marketing
similarly try to avoid PTO the week of a major launch
(`marketing/launch-checklist-q3-webinar.md`) if you own a piece of it —
this is a norm, not a rule, coordinate with your manager.

## Parental leave

16 weeks paid for the primary caregiver, 8 weeks for a secondary
caregiver, both fully separate from PTO balance. Contact Henry (People)
to start the process as soon as you have a date, ideally 2+ months ahead
for coverage planning.

## Questions

Henry owns this policy; exceptions (e.g. an unpaid sabbatical) go through
him and then Brian for sign-off.
