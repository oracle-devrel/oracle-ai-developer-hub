# Deal desk policy

Deal desk policy: reps can offer up to 20% off list on an annual contract
without approval. Anything past 20% needs VP of Sales sign-off. Multi-year
contracts can go to 30% with the same VP approval — see
`sales/discount-matrix.md` for the full table by contract length and
payment terms.

## Approval workflow

1. Rep requests the discount in the CRM opportunity, tagging deal desk.
2. Deal desk checks the discount matrix and the account's lead source —
   webinar-sourced leads (see `marketing/launch-checklist-q3-webinar.md`
   for the current UTM scheme used to identify them) get the same
   thresholds, no special treatment.
3. Anything past 20% routes to Brian for sign-off, target 1 business day
   turnaround.
4. Approved discounts get logged with the opportunity ID so finance can
   tie the discount to the invoice at close — see
   `finance/revenue-recognition-notes.md` for why that matters for revenue
   recognition timing.

## Non-standard terms

Payment terms other than the standard annual-upfront (e.g. quarterly
billing, Net-60) need finance sign-off separately from the discount
approval — see `finance/vendor-payment-terms.md`, which covers both our
vendor terms and the customer-facing exceptions process, since the same
finance reviewer handles both.

## Competitive deals

If a prospect is actively evaluating a competitor, reference the relevant
battlecard (`sales/competitive-battlecard-ledgerly.md` or
`sales/competitive-battlecard-billsworth.md`) before discounting further —
most competitive losses in H1 2026 were on implementation speed, not
price, so a discount alone rarely closes a competitive deal.
