# Discount matrix

Full table backing `sales/deal-desk-policy.md`. All figures are off list
price.

| Contract length | Payment terms | Max discount (no approval) | Max discount (VP sign-off) |
| --------------- | ------------- | -------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Annual          | Upfront       | 20%                        | 25%                        |
| Annual          | Quarterly     | 15%                        | 20%                        |
| 2-year          | Upfront       | 25%                        | 30%                        |
| 3-year          | Upfront       | 25%                        | 30%                        |

Notes:

- "No approval" means the rep can quote it directly in the CRM; anything
  above that row's ceiling requires Brian's sign-off per
  `sales/deal-desk-policy.md`.
- Quarterly payment terms cap 5 points lower than upfront at every
  contract length — cash flow timing matters more to finance than the
  headline discount, see `finance/revenue-recognition-notes.md`.
- These numbers are internal only. `marketing/brand-voice-guide.md`
  explicitly bans publishing the 30% multi-year ceiling anywhere
  prospect-facing.
- Non-profit and education accounts get a separate 10% baseline discount
  stacked on top of the above, capped at 35% total regardless of contract
  length — ask deal desk, this table doesn't cover that path.

## Revision history

- 2026-02: multi-year ceiling raised from 25% to 30% after losing two
  competitive 3-year deals to Ledgerly on price alone.
- 2025-11: quarterly-terms discount penalty introduced (previously
  quarterly and upfront had the same ceiling) after finance flagged the
  cash-flow impact.
